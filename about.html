About Prasun Games

Platform Overview

Welcome to Prasun Games, an independent digital platform dedicated to high-performance, browser-based web games created and maintained by Prasun Barua.

Our Mission

Our objective is to engineer clean, fast, and accessible gaming experiences that load instantly inside any modern desktop web browser. There are no burdensome app installations, restrictive paywalls, or mandatory sign-up walls required—simply open your browser and play.

The Technology

Starting with our flagship 3D Chess implementation powered by modern WebGL, Three.js, and official FIDE rule logic, Prasun Games focuses on fluid technical optimization, elegant user interfaces, and clean code architecture.

Future Growth

Prasun Games is actively expanding its catalog. Alongside tactical strategy titles like chess, upcoming releases will feature arcade, logic, and puzzle-oriented web games.

To explore more professional projects or get in touch with the creator directly, visit prasunbarua.com.
