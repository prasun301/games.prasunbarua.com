Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

At Prasun Games, accessible from games.prasunbarua.com, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Prasun Games and how we utilize it.

1. Log Files

Prasun Games follows a standard protocol of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any data that is personally identifiable.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like other websites, Prasun Games uses cookies to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed. This data optimizes users' experiences by customizing our web content based on browser types and other parameters.

3. Google AdSense and DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is one of our primary third-party vendors on this site. It uses cookies, including DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our website and other sites across the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Our Advertising Partners

Some advertising partners on our site may use cookies and web beacons. Below is our primary advertising partner, which maintains its own Privacy Policy regarding user data:

  • Google AdSense: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the CCPA, California consumers have rights including requesting that a business disclose categories and specific pieces of personal data collected, requesting deletion of personal data, and requesting that consumer personal data not be sold.

6. GDPR Data Protection Rights

We ensure users are fully aware of data protection rights, including the right to access, rectify, or erase personal data under certain conditions. To exercise these rights, please contact us via our Contact Page.

7. Children's Information

Protecting children online is another priority. We encourage parents and guardians to monitor and guide their online activity. Prasun Games does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

Contact Information

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact Prasun Barua via our Contact page.
