How to Play & Keyboard Controls

Standard American Rules

  • Pieces & Movement: Red starts at the bottom and moves upward. Black starts at the top and moves downward. Normal pieces (men) move one square diagonally forward.
  • Mandatory Captures: If a jump capture is available anywhere on the board, you must capture. Non-capturing moves are blocked until all captures are resolved.
  • Multi-Jumps: If a piece lands on a square where another jump is possible, it must continue jumping in the same turn.
  • King Promotion: When a piece reaches the opponent's back row, it becomes a King. Kings can move and capture both forward and backward. If a piece reaches the king row via a capture, its turn ends immediately upon promotion.
  • Winning: Win by capturing all opposing pieces or leaving your opponent with no legal moves.

Official Checkers Rules

This edition implements standard American Checkers rules (also known as English Draughts):

  • Board Layout: The game is played on an 8×8 grid containing 64 squares. Gameplay takes place exclusively on the 32 dark squares.
  • Starting Positions: Each player starts with 12 pieces placed on the dark squares of the three rows closest to their side. Red moves first.
  • Jumping & Capturing: You capture an opposing piece by jumping over it diagonally to an unoccupied square immediately behind it. Captured pieces are removed from the board.
  • Forced Jumps: Capturing is mandatory. If one or more jumps are available on your turn, you cannot make a standard diagonal move.
  • Kingship: Reaching the farthest row promotes your piece to a King, granting it the power to move and jump both forward and backward.

History of Checkers & Draughts

Checkers is one of mankind's oldest surviving board games. Historians trace its origins to an ancient game called Alquerque, played thousands of years ago in the Middle East. Around the 12th century in France, Alquerque was adapted to the standard 64-square chessboard, forming the foundation of modern Draughts. By the 18th century, rulebooks standardized English Draughts, which evolved into American Checkers—a game celebrated today for its deceptive simplicity and rich strategic depth.

Checkers Strategy Tips

  1. Control the Center: Dominate the middle squares of the board to maximize your movement options and restrict your opponent's mobility.
  2. Protect Your Back Row: Keep your back row intact for as long as possible to prevent your opponent from easily promoting their pieces to Kings.
  3. Trade Advantageously: When you hold a piece advantage, trading pieces evenly simplifies the board and accelerates your path to victory.
  4. Set Up Traps: Force your opponent into mandatory captures that open up multi-jump opportunities for your own pieces.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this Checkers game free to play?

Yes! Checkers on Prasun Games is completely free to play directly in your web browser with no registration or payment required.

Are captures mandatory in this game?

Yes. In standard American Checkers rules, if a jump capture is available on your turn, you must take it.

Can Kings move backward?

Yes. Unlike standard men, Kings can move and jump diagonally in both forward and backward directions.

What happens if a player has no legal moves left?

Under official American Checkers rules, if a player cannot make any legal moves on their turn, they lose the game.
