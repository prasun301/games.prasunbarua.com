Enjoy a classic game of Checkers with this free browser-based version from Prasun Games. The game is designed for two players sharing the same device, making it easy to play with a friend on a desktop, tablet, or mobile phone.

Checkers is a strategy board game played on an 8×8 board. Players move their pieces diagonally across the dark squares and capture opposing pieces by jumping over them.

Checkers Rules

Each player begins with twelve pieces. Regular pieces move diagonally forward by one square. When an opponent's piece is directly beyond an available diagonal square, the piece can jump over it and capture it.

Captures are mandatory in this version of the game. When another capture is available immediately after a jump, the same piece must continue jumping until no additional capture is available.

A piece that reaches the opponent's back row is promoted to a king. Kings can move and capture diagonally in both directions.

How to Win at Checkers

The objective is to capture all of your opponent's pieces or leave them without a legal move. Good Checkers strategy involves protecting your pieces, creating forced captures, controlling the board, and planning multiple jumps several moves ahead.
