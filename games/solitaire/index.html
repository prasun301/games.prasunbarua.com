Classic Solitaire Online

Solitaire is one of the world's most popular single-player card games. This free online version uses the classic Klondike Solitaire rules and can be played directly in your web browser.

How to Play Solitaire

The objective of Klondike Solitaire is to move all 52 cards into the four foundation piles. Each foundation is built from Ace through King in the same suit.

On the main tableau, cards are arranged in descending order while alternating between red and black suits. Face-down cards are turned over when the face-up card covering them is moved.

Solitaire Rules

  • Build the four foundation piles from Ace to King.
  • Cards in the tableau are arranged in descending order.
  • Tableau cards must alternate between red and black suits.
  • Only a King can be placed into an empty tableau column.
  • Face-down cards are revealed when uncovered.
  • The stock provides additional cards when you need them.

Win at Solitaire

Plan your moves carefully and try to reveal face-down cards whenever possible. Moving cards from the tableau to the foundation can be useful, but sometimes keeping a card in the tableau gives you more options.
