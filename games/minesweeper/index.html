How to Play Minesweeper

Minesweeper is a puzzle game where your objective is to reveal all safe cells on a grid without detonating any hidden mines.

  • Reveal Cells: Click or tap on any cell to reveal what lies beneath.
  • Read Number Clues: Numbers on revealed cells indicate how many mines exist in the adjacent 8 surrounding tiles.
  • Flag Suspected Mines: Right-click or use Flag Mode on touch screens to place a protective flag on tiles you believe contain a mine.
  • Chain Reveals: Revealing an empty cell automatically uncovers adjacent safe areas to speed up your game.
  • Win the Game: Uncover every non-mine tile on the board to win.

Minesweeper Rules

  • The first click is always safe and will never detonate a mine.
  • Numbers calculate adjacent mines vertically, horizontally, and diagonally.
  • If a revealed mine detonates, the game immediately ends in defeat.
  • Flags are strategic tools to help you track dangerous tiles; placing flags on all mines is not strictly required to trigger victory as long as all safe cells are revealed.
