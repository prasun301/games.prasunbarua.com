How to Play Minesweeper

Minesweeper is a puzzle game where your objective is to reveal all safe cells on a grid without detonating any hidden mines.

  • Reveal Cells: Click or tap on any cell to reveal what lies beneath.
  • Read Number Clues: Numbers indicate how many mines are present in the surrounding eight cells.
  • Flag Suspected Mines: Right-click or use Flag Mode on touch devices to mark cells that you believe contain mines.
  • Chain Reveals: Empty cells automatically reveal nearby safe areas.
  • Win the Game: Reveal every non-mine cell on the board.

Minesweeper Rules

  • The first click is always safe.
  • Numbers represent adjacent mines vertically, horizontally, and diagonally.
  • Revealing a mine ends the game.
  • Flags help you track suspected mines but are not required for victory.
