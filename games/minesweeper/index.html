How to Play Minesweeper

  • Click any cell to reveal it.
  • Numbers show how many mines are hidden in the surrounding cells.
  • Right-click a cell, or use Flag Mode on mobile, to mark it as a suspected mine.
  • Use the number clues to work out which nearby cells are safe.
  • Avoid revealing a cell that hides a mine.
  • Reveal every safe cell on the board to win.

Minesweeper Rules

  • A set number of mines are hidden at random across the board.
  • Each revealed number shows how many mines are in the eight surrounding cells.
  • Diagonal cells count as adjacent when counting nearby mines.
  • Flags help you track cells you suspect hide a mine, but placing them is optional.
  • You win by revealing every cell that does not hide a mine.
  • Revealing a mine immediately ends the game.
